# 01 — Product and progression requirements

## Product definition

**PR-01:** TFT is a single-player, offline-capable incremental game built for web and touch-first play. A player begins with one tree in the void and gradually builds a world-scale economy. They can actively accelerate growth or let unlocked producers operate while away. The fantasy is turning manual effort into renewable supply and ultimately an industrial machine producing the terminal resource. Players should always know a meaningful next goal and why earlier resources are still needed.

**PR-02:** The first public playable version includes Orchard, Workshop, Farm, Quarry, Arena, Factory and Power Grid with observable dependencies. No separate 3D environment, spatial physics, character movement, real-world manufacturing simulation, multiplayer, trading, paid currency, login or cloud save in v1. A PWA may be installed from the browser but installation isn't required to play. Data stays local; no analytics collection or ad SDK in v1.

**PR-03:** Active input accelerates timers only; never mint an item per tap. Unlocks grant an actual capability or remove an actual bottleneck. Automation is incremental: manual buffered growth, mechanical collection, mechanical timer assist, electric production, routing, high-power industrial processing. No prestige needed to obtain the first automatic producer or any required technology. The active and passive economies use identical recipe definitions.

**PR-04:** Materials are functional inputs, not just points. Technology is a one-time recipe (cost + all required tech). Repeatable constructions pay their specified price. Advancement badges never gate required technology except a directly stated capability (e.g. Arena mastery gates a grinder, which is documented on the grinder purchase itself). No random required material with unbounded wait. **PR-05:** Shared storage begins at a hard 160-item ceiling and expands through six material-gated modular technology tiers. Storage is a mandatory progression branch, not a setting or only a cosmetic local buffer. See [storage capacity progression](06-storage.md).

## Progression structure

Use the exact IDs from [the catalog](02-catalog.md). Descriptive phase names are UI grouping and mastery boards, **not** a requirement to complete every side quest. Gate purchases are in Workshop; all producer tasks run in their appropriate tab. If an item is not yet available, its recipe card shows the direct source and alternate first-acquisition route. First discoveries consume earlier obtainable inputs; known cultivars persist for that world. Any route in this document that uses a capability implies the costs in the catalog; do not grant it for free on phase entry.

### Phase 0 — Rootwork (new player / vertical slice)

- RG-01: Start Orchard and Workshop unlocked, one starter plot planted, all stock zero, 160 shared item capacity. First harvest at 12s if idle; one ready buffer advances without active taps. Active tutorial points at character, then timer and collect; skippable, no forced clicking.
- RG-02: Convert two litter to one dirt, pay three dirt + two timber to add a second plot. Show plot count and price; same species can be planted in both. Cannot remove the last plot or lose access to Starter.
- RG-03: Screen purchase (4 timber + 2 dirt), process 1 dirt → 2 grit, spend grit plus cutting on Stonewood cultivar. Claywood requires stone, dirt and cutting. No random mandatory screen loot, no need for a material the player is trying to unlock.
- RG-04: Clay opens the 4 clay/4 timber/2 stone mechanical collector. Attach to an individual plot, auto-transfer output. It uses no electrical power; natural growth still works if detached. The next upgrade is a distinct clockwork auto-twerker (2 clay, 2 timber, 1 stone), reducing duration by 20%. This is the first demonstration of auto-twerking in the pitch. Reassign attachments freely when target is available.
- RG-05: First starter harvest may expose the Field seed, probability 20% on each of first two completions if absent; guarantee third. The seed is unique per world, spent only on Farm. It is credited to its harvest buffer and not lost when full or switching plot. A player can ignore it and complete roots first.
- RG-06: Slice capstone: three known cultivars, at least two plots, one collector and one clockwork auto-twerker installed concurrently, ten cumulative clay units automatically collected since first collector purchase (not current stock). Screen success and first collector milestones appear when actually acquired. Roots can continue indefinitely, and a locked next-tier card previews the kiln in a slice-only build.
- RG-07: Initial pacing witness without prestige or active input: plot2 36s → screen/stone 60s → clay 78s → plot3 96s → collector 136s → clockwork 156s → 10 auto clay at 220s. This assumes prompt collection/instant bench crafting. These are target calculations, not a mandatory timer or measured playtest duration. Root arithmetic peak is at most 131 item units including possible seed, so the 160 base cap preserves the original route. Wooden bins are available immediately from timber/dirt; not required to complete the tiny root slice. Root-playtest targets: first harvest within 12s, first expanded plot within 1 minute under clear tutorial, first collector within 2–5 minutes passive collection or 1–3 minutes active. Rebalance only after observation.

### Storage branch (parallel, mandatory by industrial convergence)

- Start with 160 shared inventory units. Build first wooden bins (+160) from timber/dirt, clay silo (+640), metal warehouse (+2,560), network rack (+10,240), crystal vault (+40,960), folded repository (+163,840). Each module has explicit recipe/gates and four total capacity per tier; earlier tiers remain constructible. A single module of each gives 218,560, four each gives 873,760. See [complete storage tree](06-storage.md).
- Full global warehouse holds producer bundles and completed machine outputs instead of deleting or minting items. Local buffers remain separate. Storage expands when physically built, not on knowledge alone. All modules stay accessible without power; production/transfer still obeys Grid.
- Sorting network requires clay silo; networked rack requires refined alloy and control. Press requires rack (13,760 minimum with one each through tier four). Crystal/folded modules are optional extensions for Endgame/Endless. Storage milestones are on Supply Lines board.

### Phase 1 — Cultivation and metallurgy

- CV-01: Field seed + required materials unlock Farm with one field. Select crop task (18s, crop/fibre/waste). Crop grows without feed or power; other fields need construction. Crop → ration for Arena; crop → feed for pen. Compost joins litter/waste and unlocks shorter growth. Pen consumes a feed, gives animal produce; rich ration route optional. Oil field requires iron tools/compost and makes oil/waste/fiber; oil is mandatory later for polymers and renewable energy option.
- CV-02: Buy kiln from clay/stone/timber. Manual charcoal from timber, fired parts from clay/charcoal, starter copper and iron concentrates from current materials, then smelt with charcoal or later coal. Starter charcoal job does not require preexisting fuel; this is the black-start baseline.
- CV-03: Coal cultivar graft uses fired part, charcoal and stone. Basic copper ingot then copperwood, iron ingot then ironwood, tin via copper + stone/fired part then tinwood, silica from screened grit + clay then sandwood. Each graft consumes its cost once and guarantees named raw output thereafter. The original low-rate bootstrap can be reused after grafting, allowing recovery. Tin/bronze unlock methods are recipes, not loot drops.
- CV-04: Alloy heat lining enables manual bronze from refined copper/tin. Forged tools upgrade Orchard/Quarry yields. Iron pick opens Quarry + first work gang; source recipes directly show that shallow copper/iron/tin/coal/silica/stone beds are now renewable. Quarry adds economical high-throughput alternative, **not** forced replacement of tree production. Quartz bed opens via iron/fired parts/stone, and graft unlocks quartz tree.
- CV-05: Basic food and materials unlock Arena; starting enemy supplies core. Reinforced armor opens Stoneback, quartz-era trial unlocks Glowbug, later Titan requires advanced alloy and precision line. First manually won encounter always gives a normal guaranteed drop plus one-time bonus; actual success is probabilistic with pity after two losses. On death, cooldown, no inventory/equipment destruction. All mandatory drops have guaranteed quantities once a victory occurs. Arena is a real input source, not an optional cosmetic minigame.
- CV-06: Resinwood requires Farm fiber/compost and copper; gives adhesive/resin for controls and polymers. Farm feeds orchard growth and industry; livestock and extra fields are optional scale routes.

### Phase 2 — Power and manufacturing

- PW-01: Purchase Grid using iron/copper/fired parts creates first combustion generator. Fuel options are timber, charcoal, coal. It is explicitly possible to generate electricity without fuel already produced by powered machines. Generator UI gives output W and burn duration; battery purchase available after silica/glass. Manual tasks and mechanical attachments still run with Grid empty.
- PW-02: Factory unlock creates first powered processor. Electric metal recipe yields two ingots from 2 raw; manual recipe yields one with fuel. First processor can be manually started, then repeat after Sorting network (requires clay storage silo). Power is global; every running powered operation obeys allocation rules. No fake output during outage.
- PW-03: Powered alloy furnace, sorting network, circuit bench and controls are purchased with available base metals/glass/quartz/resin. Routing unlocks machine repeating, quotas and demand crafting; controls unlock powered field/pen harvesters, drills and twerkers. Upgrades can reduce cycle duration at power cost or lower watt cost without changing yield.
- PW-04: Ward motor uses Arena core and control parts; automatic battles require food, HP, watts, enemy unlock. After five manual/auto victories per enemy, a purchased grinder can be assigned; deterministic no-HP simulation yields that enemy's normal drops with ration input and power. Never use a first-win bonus repeatedly. Grinder purchase itself requires at least one mastered opponent; changing assignment does not grant mastery for another enemy.
- PW-05: Deep ore bed opens with bronze, quartz and iron before advanced alloy or advanced drill. Heavy ore supplies advanced alloys. Gem bed uses quartz, iron and Burrower core. Heavywood/Gemwood optional slower renewable backups. Deep drill increases volume after alloy/control/shell investment, not a circular requirement to unlock its own materials.
- PW-06: Polymer consumes plant oil, resin and fired part; precision line uses quartz/gem/control (later shard alternate); stabilizer consumes glow dust/shell/compost; fermentation consumes crop/waste for biofuel. High-capacity generator requires alloys/crystal/control and can still burn regular coal. Mineral/precision soil and yield upgrades give meaningful optional acceleration. Crystalwood gives an alternate precision input only after stabilizer/gem discovery; its shard route must not be necessary to obtain the first crystal.

### Phase 3 — Convergence / full game capstone

- CG-01: Titan trial (high-tier equipment) yields rare core. Convergence unlock costs alloy, polymer, crystal, catalyst, rare core and prior lines; its timed recipe produces convergence core from those resources. Build Worldseed Press with the core, alloy/control/crystal plus advanced Grid and networked storage rack. One first station per unlock; extra presses pay full build price.
- CG-02: Every Press batch takes 30s, draws 20 W, consumes **2 alloy + 2 polymer + 2 crystal + 1 catalyst + 1 compost**, makes exactly 1 Worldseed. This implies all seven zones across its supply chain. Worldseed occupies the shared storage ceiling and is protected from ordinary discard. It cannot be sold or converted to another resource; after victory only, explicit archive retires current stock without payout or altering cumulative record. The cumulative completed-output counter (not owned stock) defines victory. With two per minute the 600-unit candidate threshold requires five ideal hours of uninterrupted final production after ramp-up.
- CG-03: Press status includes current rate (completed output trailing real simulation minute; empty initial shows "calculating"), planned capacity, remaining to threshold and ETA if recent rate > 0. If paused, show exact blocker and route to the missing resource/energy; no invented ETA.
- CG-04: A validated sustainable example is encoded in catalog: source slots, exact rational average recipes, two 100 W generators burning no more than two coal/min, worst simultaneous sum 180 W. Minimum rack capacity 13,760; with 3,000 starting stock after 300 minutes the average-rate estimate is 8,842 units including all positive net byproducts and terminal output. It is a capacity witness with setup stock, not an optimal build or a guarantee of zero idle timing stalls. Initial setup should let players see why merely adding presses without suppliers does not accelerate output. Show reserved/available stock, planned demand, and actual production separately.
- CG-05: At 600 cumulative completed Worldseed, mark world won exactly once, grant one prestige point exactly once and record win timestamp and current world statistics. No forced reset, no looping reward from re-opened modal, no requirement for every optional technology or board. Run state remains usable.
- CG-06: On a completed world, select Endless or Replant. Endless retains everything and continues terminal output, records and remaining mastery. Replant is an explicit, previewed, confirmed operation, enabled after win only in full mode. A prototype-only scaled root-trial mode may test a smaller new-world reward separately (never unlock full release victories or import as them).

## Prestige and world lifecycle (exact candidate)

- Starting profile: zero points, levels and historical records, fresh world ID and seed. Win grants one point once per unique completed world. Available points are earned minus spent. Spend only in the replant preview screen, by selecting an affordable upgrade, or carry unspent points. Cancel leaves all state unchanged. Purchase and world creation occur atomically with the replant confirmation.
- Four perks, each maximum level 3, each level costs 1 point: extra starting dirt (+2 per level); starter tree duration ×0.9 per level (only starter tree); additional starting manual buffer cycles (+1 per level, subject to cap); extra starting empty plot (+1 per level, at most 64 total plots). Combine starter duration with normal upgrades only after new world unlocks them. No perk blocks or changes tech graph gates.
- On Replant preserve profile ID, earned/spent, perk levels, settings, cosmetics/mastery badges designated profile-wide, previous world's summary (world ID, start/win, elapsed online/offline, maximum Worldseed rate, final count), and a backup export opportunity. Reset inventory except starting perk stock, cultivars except Starter, zones to Orchard/Workshop, all tech/builds/jobs/fuel/storage modules and stock policies (reset to 160 capacity)/HP/enemy mastery and RNG seed. Create a new unique world ID, seed and fresh world counters. Keep last 20 world summaries plus aggregate records (not old active games); no secret parallel-world generation.
- No reset of profile except separately confirmed "erase all local data" with backup export. On cap all perks, unspent points may accumulate for records; no hidden exponential additional upgrades.

## Optional mastery boards

Milestones are persisted world facts, auto-completed once; never charge or consume inputs to claim them. Rootwork (first harvest/dirt/plot/stone/clay/collector/clockwork/10 auto clay), Cultivation (field/crop/compost/pen/oil), Metallurgy (kiln/coal/copper/iron/tin/bronze/quarry), Supply Lines (six storage tiers, full warehouse/recovery, local buffer then collect/recovery, router, first powered repeat, first sustained batch), Spark (Grid, fuel type, battery, overdraw recovery, advanced generator), Fieldwork (quartz/deep/gem beds, Arena first wins, five wins/mastery, grinder output), Convergence (polymer/crystal/catalyst/core/Press/600). UI shows progress/next action; optional completion stats and cosmetic badge descriptions. No extra prestige points for boards in v1. Persist facts per world; profiles record wins and board completion history.

Every enabled technology, build, cultivar, task, enemy and generator is specified in the [catalog](02-catalog.md); it is the numerical implementation contract. Review requirement changes against the [engine behavior](03-simulation-and-state.md) and [acceptance matrix](05-acceptance-and-roadmap.md).
