# Sky Factory 4 — progression systems research

**Research scope:** conventional SkyFactory 4 skyblock, Minecraft 1.12.2, with the optional Prestige mode called out separately. Reviewed 2026-11-27. This is a design analysis, not a walkthrough or a claim that we ran the modpack. The earlier [partial advancement transcription](inspiration-sky-factory-4.md) remains intact as historical reference.

## Primary evidence and version boundaries

The pack author explicitly describes the resource trees as a replacement for random Ex Nihilo-style resource acquisition, and the advancement system as optional direction rather than a compulsory quest chain. Prestige is an **optional world-creation setting**; reward purchases carry across worlds. See the [author's distribution page](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/modpacks/skyfactory-4) and [official repository](https://github.com/DarkPacks/SkyFactory-4). The [distribution listing](https://atlauncher.com/pack/SkyFactory4) lists 4.2.4; the pack's public source snapshot below is the master commit of 2023-08-29, `6bbfe9681e7443eeef2143723430614a5f8e20c1`. It is evidence of shipped-design intent and configuration, not a byte-verified 4.2.4 installation. World presets (including Pacifist, compact islands and Sky Grid) alter some recipes/criteria. Do not conflate their availability or pretend every parent edge is a hard recipe prerequisite.

Reviewed actual [localized descriptions](https://github.com/DarkPacks/SkyFactory-4/blob/6bbfe9681e7443eeef2143723430614a5f8e20c1/src/resources/skyfactory4/lang/en_us.lang), [Triumph advancement definitions](https://github.com/DarkPacks/SkyFactory-4/tree/6bbfe9681e7443eeef2143723430614a5f8e20c1/src/config/triumph/script/sf4), [Sky Orchards recipe customizations](https://github.com/DarkPacks/SkyFactory-4/blob/6bbfe9681e7443eeef2143723430614a5f8e20c1/src/scripts/crafttweaker/recipes/mods/skyOrchards.zs), [casting](https://github.com/DarkPacks/SkyFactory-4/blob/6bbfe9681e7443eeef2143723430614a5f8e20c1/src/scripts/crafttweaker/modIntegrations/tinkers.zs), [cauldron conversions](https://github.com/DarkPacks/SkyFactory-4/blob/6bbfe9681e7443eeef2143723430614a5f8e20c1/src/scripts/crafttweaker/modIntegrations/inspirations.zs), [crushing](https://github.com/DarkPacks/SkyFactory-4/blob/6bbfe9681e7443eeef2143723430614a5f8e20c1/src/scripts/crafttweaker/modIntegrations/rustic.zs), [resource-family registration](https://github.com/DarkPacks/SkyFactory-4/blob/6bbfe9681e7443eeef2143723430614a5f8e20c1/src/scripts/crafttweaker/resources/skyOrchards.zs), [prestige reward graph](https://github.com/DarkPacks/SkyFactory-4/blob/6bbfe9681e7443eeef2143723430614a5f8e20c1/src/config/prestige/rewards.json), and [Extended Crafting configuration](https://github.com/DarkPacks/SkyFactory-4/blob/6bbfe9681e7443eeef2143723430614a5f8e20c1/src/config/extendedcrafting.cfg). These are stronger evidence than the prior manually pasted wiki entries. Base mod recipes not overridden in the pack may live outside this repository; no claim of a complete recipe dump.

## 1. The early progression is a branching resource graph

The first tree is Dirt, not a generic mining action. Repeated growth acceleration, leaves, logs, acorns, resin and saplings bootstrap the productive economy: assemble new land from tree output; use a crook to recover saplings; preserve and expand seed stock. Drying, crushing, fluid conversion, and heating are deliberately different operations. New saplings are cultivated by transforming older saplings with things the player can already make. Resources are renewable after the gate, rather than repeated discoveries.

A representative route from actual scripted goals and recipes:

| Unlock family | What is actually evidenced | Design lesson |
|---|---|---|
| Dirt to petrified | The [petrified advancement](https://github.com/DarkPacks/SkyFactory-4/blob/6bbfe9681e7443eeef2143723430614a5f8e20c1/src/config/triumph/script/sf4/age1/sapling/petrified.txt) points at dirt and drying; the shaped recipe combines petrified acorns with a dirt sapling. | A first processing tool unlocks a specific renewable material. |
| Gravel, sand and clay | Gravel's guide parents include petrified and crushing; sand follows gravel in the guide but the recipe uses petrified sapling and sand acorns. Clay's alternative recipe combines dirt sapling and resin from dirt and petrified trees. | Guide order is illustrative; actual ingredients are the dependency graph. Multiple same-tier offshoots keep old trees relevant. |
| Bone and coal | Bone follows petrified and crushing; a recipe uses bone meal on a petrified sapling. Coal's advancement follows sand. | Introduce fertilizer and fuel alongside structural materials. |
| Water, lava, obsidian | Cauldron or crushing surplus saplings provides water; heated cauldron transforms stone to lava; water and lava produce obsidian. Bucket progression makes new processing possible. | Tools change which transformations can be performed; fluids are outputs, not merely additional currencies. |
| First iron | Iron amber has recipes combining **coal, gravel, sand, clay resins** before an iron tree exists. A melter produces liquid iron; the pack casts that metal onto an acacia sapling. Once established, iron-tree acorns and resin supply iron amber repeatedly. | Require a one-time cross-resource bootstrap then open a cheaper repeatable production route. Never require a tree's own drop to unlock the first instance. |
| Further metals | Shaped bootstrap recipes combine earlier resin families: gold uses iron, lead and sand plus dye; silver uses petrified, lead, tin plus dye; copper, tin and nickel have their own combinations. Casting on different sapling types establishes iron, tin, copper, lead, gold, silver, nickel, osmium and later metals. | Unlock recipes should consume different resource families and a processing capability; high-tier output should not be obtainable by merely waiting longer on a starter plot. |
| Redstone, lapis, diamonds, emerald, quartz | Redstone recipe includes iron and petrified resin on coal sapling; diamond uses silver, gold and lapis resin on redstone sapling; emerald uses nickel and tin with a diamond sapling and material reagent. Quartz and glowstone add additional special-material dependencies. | Late starter trees combine multiple branch accomplishments instead of a simple linear metal ladder. |
| Food, animal and specialist materials | Cottonwood supplies string; crops, animal seeds, hogs and alternative tree types expand beyond ores. Recipe scripts include novelty food saplings and catalysts from ordinary foods. | Make side systems productive, funny and economically useful; not every reward has to be an ore. |

Sources: linked recipes/casting/cauldron/crushing above; [starter goal files](https://github.com/DarkPacks/SkyFactory-4/tree/6bbfe9681e7443eeef2143723430614a5f8e20c1/src/config/triumph/script/sf4/age1). Several crafted alternatives use an acorn of the target tree to recover or duplicate a sapling; they are **not** its first unlock route. The resource classes distinguish logs/leaves/saplings, acorns/resin, optional amber and melted liquids; the default metal amber liquid amount is marked as ore-doubling in the [class](https://github.com/DarkPacks/SkyFactory-4/blob/6bbfe9681e7443eeef2143723430614a5f8e20c1/src/scripts/crafttweaker/classes/resources/skyOrchardsType.zs). Some families explicitly remove amber (e.g. clay, gravel, diamond). Not all materials follow one universal recipe.

## 2. Seven advancement boards, not seven linear locks

The pinned tree contains 7 board directories and 256 definition files (counts below include roots, mode-specific capstones and hidden/world-type variants; **not** visible achievement counts). The following table summarizes their gameplay role, not the original names/descriptions of individual goals.

| Board (source directory) | Definitions | Representative progression | TFT translation |
|---|---:|---|---|
| [Starter](https://github.com/DarkPacks/SkyFactory-4/tree/6bbfe9681e7443eeef2143723430614a5f8e20c1/src/config/triumph/script/sf4/age1) | 56 | Plant materials, expand dirt, tools, processing vessels, tree graph, initial metal and diamond, fuel and liquid loops. | Orchard roots and Workshop milestone board; one satisfying automation payoff before expansion. |
| [Enhancement](https://github.com/DarkPacks/SkyFactory-4/tree/6bbfe9681e7443eeef2143723430614a5f8e20c1/src/config/triumph/script/sf4/age_enhancement) | 33 | Tool parts and modifiers, metal melters/alloy tank, machine speed and energy upgrades, armor, health and inventory upgrades. | Speed/yield/efficiency as separate axes (not just one production multiplier). |

| [Exploration](https://github.com/DarkPacks/SkyFactory-4/tree/6bbfe9681e7443eeef2143723430614a5f8e20c1/src/config/triumph/script/sf4/age_exploration) | 28 | Dimension gates, unique resources, alternate sources for ores, enemies, compact workspaces and travel capability. | Unlock Quarry strata and Arena opponents through workshops; additional ways to produce rare materials, not a separate 3D exploration game. |
| [Late collection](https://github.com/DarkPacks/SkyFactory-4/tree/6bbfe9681e7443eeef2143723430614a5f8e20c1/src/config/triumph/script/sf4/age_allthethingsomglol) | 33 | Reactor benchmarks, very large storage, all-resource challenges, compression and final singularities. | A measurable output capstone plus optional mastery goals; do not require every collection goal to win. |

The actual [starter capstone](https://github.com/DarkPacks/SkyFactory-4/blob/6bbfe9681e7443eeef2143723430614a5f8e20c1/src/config/triumph/script/sf4/age1/complete.txt) explicitly requires parents and awards prestige via a function only when the prestige stage is active. The [farming board](https://github.com/DarkPacks/SkyFactory-4/blob/6bbfe9681e7443eeef2143723430614a5f8e20c1/src/config/triumph/script/sf4/age_farming/complete.txt) similarly has an alternate [non-prestige capstone](https://github.com/DarkPacks/SkyFactory-4/blob/6bbfe9681e7443eeef2143723430614a5f8e20c1/src/config/triumph/script/sf4/age_farming/complete_no_prestige.txt) with different conditions/rewards. Some localized labels misleadingly mention prestige even in non-prestige variants; definitions govern. TFT should use a board of discoverable goals independent of the actual recipe/technology graph; unlocking a recipe should never require an unrelated badge. Use smaller themed goals and one capstone in each board rather than reproducing all 256 files.

## 3. Automation is a ladder, not a single purchase

A normal bonsai pot still needs manual harvesting with a tool; its hopping counterpart sends output to inventories automatically ([farming advancement descriptions](https://github.com/DarkPacks/SkyFactory-4/blob/6bbfe9681e7443eeef2143723430614a5f8e20c1/src/resources/skyfactory4/lang/en_us.lang), [hopping definition](https://github.com/DarkPacks/SkyFactory-4/blob/6bbfe9681e7443eeef2143723430614a5f8e20c1/src/config/triumph/script/sf4/age_farming/hoppingbonsai.txt)). That still leaves raw drops to be processed. Storage upgrades and routing matter when output saturates. The powered automatic packager assembles amber and other selected blocks; machine melting, casting, drying and alloy production form separate transformations. The loop's next bottleneck moves from **harvest → storage → processing → power → rarer inputs → throughput**.

[Actual soil configs](https://github.com/DarkPacks/SkyFactory-4/tree/6bbfe9681e7443eeef2143723430614a5f8e20c1/src/config/bonsaitrees/soils.d). The index is a comparison of multipliers, **not a measured real output multiplier** (base drop chances, caps and rounding may matter). The prestige text for brown says 125% drop chance; its config is 1.4, so do not blindly trust reward text for balance. The main idea is repeated investment in a producer that remains useful long after its discovery. TFT should initially use smaller throughput increments and separate soil upgrades (cycle duration), tools (yield), automation (collection/routing) and power efficiency. Avoid multiplying every axis indefinitely.

## 4. Farming, mining, combat, storage and power feed each other

- **Farming:** seeds can be created from grass or leaf processing; crops and irrigation support repeatable yields. The pack also permits animal seeds/lilies and resource hogs on matching soils, with different resource-specific truffles ([resource hog configs](https://github.com/DarkPacks/SkyFactory-4/tree/6bbfe9681e7443eeef2143723430614a5f8e20c1/src/config/resourcehogs/types)). A player can manufacture renewable supply rather than repeatedly hunt it. TFT's Farm should feed the Orchard (soil and boost consumables), generator fuel and industrial reagents; crop diversity should follow a resource demand.
- **Mining/exploration:** the exploration board uses new dimensions to obtain ores not readily grown on starter trees; laser mining is an alternate route for specific materials. TFT's Quarry should increase volume and expose new strata while keeping tree yields a low-throughput fallback.
- **Combat:** Deep Mob Learning's loop is manual fights / data models progressing to simulations and resource fabrication; higher models produce more valuable returns. The pack even provides Pacifist-specific synthetic model recipes. See [power definitions](https://github.com/DarkPacks/SkyFactory-4/tree/6bbfe9681e7443eeef2143723430614a5f8e20c1/src/config/triumph/script/sf4/age_power), [mod recipe overrides](https://github.com/DarkPacks/SkyFactory-4/blob/6bbfe9681e7443eeef2143723430614a5f8e20c1/src/scripts/crafttweaker/recipes/mods/deepMobLearning.zs). TFT already makes Arena drops mandatory for named machines; keep a guaranteed manual acquisition path and later unlock a safe automated equivalent. No combat mechanics copied.
- **Storage:** storage networks reduce access friction, but don't replace production: inventory routing and processing throughput are distinct. For TFT, show consumption/production rates and full-buffer or missing-input states; keep the global inventory accessible at all times even before industrial automation.
- **Power:** the board has generators, speed modifiers, chargers, cells, machine tools, power reporting and increasingly powerful processing; enhancement explicitly trades machine speed for power and has efficiency upgrades. TFT should let a player constrain automatic work by available power and voluntarily prioritize a bottleneck instead of randomly losing output.

## 5. Prestige and the terminal resource are different systems

Prestige in SF4 is optional, global to the profile, and has two acquisition routes: completing eligible advancement boards and operating a Parabox. The author describes it as a device for time loops that grant points, not simply a mandatory full world reset. The [Parabox config](https://github.com/DarkPacks/SkyFactory-4/blob/6bbfe9681e7443eeef2143723430614a5f8e20c1/src/config/parabox.cfg) has a 12,000-tick cycle, energy settings, and a backup blacklist preserving player/advancement state; it is not a simple AFK constant-rate resource. [Recipe](https://github.com/DarkPacks/SkyFactory-4/blob/6bbfe9681e7443eeef2143723430614a5f8e20c1/src/scripts/crafttweaker/recipes/mods/parabox.zs) combines industrial components and energy storage. Its upgraded reward specifies three points per loop with higher-tier inputs including singularities. Actual runtime rollback and persistence were not tested.

Example reward graph from [rewards.json](https://github.com/DarkPacks/SkyFactory-4/blob/6bbfe9681e7443eeef2143723430614a5f8e20c1/src/config/prestige/rewards.json): hopping bonsai is the 1-point root, then starter skip (3 points), smeltery (2), soil (1) and successive soil upgrades (2–8); later crop-resource and currency-like systems can cost tens or hundreds of points. This creates a choice among permanent powers, not a flat multiplier. Do not port the optional prestige-mode restriction to TFT: its core clay auto-harvest should be available on world one.

The late-game [Quantum Compressor](https://github.com/DarkPacks/SkyFactory-4/blob/6bbfe9681e7443eeef2143723430614a5f8e20c1/src/config/triumph/script/sf4/age_allthethingsomglol/quantumcompressor.txt) requires power and a catalyst; [Extended Crafting config](https://github.com/DarkPacks/SkyFactory-4/blob/6bbfe9681e7443eeef2143723430614a5f8e20c1/src/config/extendedcrafting.cfg) describes default singularity recipes using large material and energy quantities, and a combined ultimate singularity. These are not the same as TFT's Universal Paperclips-style **one-resource win condition**. Borrow the bulk-conversion challenge, not the literal item, recipes or final checklist. TFT's terminal production should be an actual sustained output chain whose ingredients prove that the branches interconnect.

## What to keep and what to leave

| Keep | Leave or transform |
|---|---|
| Resource discovery → first transformation → renewable producer → automation | Minecraft textures, names tied to mod IP, exact recipes and copied achievement jokes |
| Parallel branches with converging prerequisites | An exhaustive modpack-like tool roster or mandatory achievement grind |
| Manual acceleration → collection automation → automatic processing → bottleneck optimization | Spatial hopper/cable routing, chunk loading, inventory minutiae |
| Optional mastery boards and progressively better soils, machine efficiency and throughput | A prestige tax before the first enjoyable passive producer |
| Combat and farming as sources of unique material catalysts | Full combat triangle or imported third-party game rules |
| High-volume compression and a meaningful endgame | Arbitrary enormous counts without visible throughput and ETA |

**Important existing-design refinement:** TFT's pitch includes a sieve. The SF4 author's explicit innovation was targeted tree production instead of randomized sieving. Keep TFT's starter screen/sieve as a short deterministic seed-discovery tool if desired: it is TFT's own mechanic, not an accurate recreation of SF4, and becomes obsolete as soon as target trees are available. The main inspiration is the intentional resource loop, not the long random grind.
