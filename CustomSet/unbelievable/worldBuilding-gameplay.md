### Gameplay Suggestions (Pending Approval)
<!-- Mechanical/gameplay ideas sparked by lore decisions. Not committed. -->
- **Story-through-cards at common/uncommon:** Each main character could have a mini narrative arc told through 2-3 cards at lower rarities in their colors, depicting their rise before their legendary form. E.g., a common "Spike, Fresh Off the Truck" → uncommon "Spike, Reading the Meta" → rare/mythic legendary "Spike, [Title]". This is similar to how Kamigawa: Neon Dynasty told the story of the Wandering Emperor or how Brothers' War depicted Urza and Mishra at different life stages.
- **"Fresh Arrival" as a card concept** could be a vanilla or French vanilla common Human — deliberately simple to show "this person has no idea what's going on yet." Could be a cycle across colors (different humans arriving different ways).
- **Isekai vehicles as a common cycle:** Truck-kun, summoning circle, portal — each could be a different card type. Truck-kun as an artifact vehicle would be very on-brand.
- **Rarity could map to timeline position:** Commons show the early, simple world. Uncommons show growing complexity and power. Rares/mythics show the climax. This is a natural fit for New World Order (complexity increases with rarity) — the timeline itself justifies the complexity ramp.
