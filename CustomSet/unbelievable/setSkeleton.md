**Reprint Limit: 15 cards maximum.** Standard expansion sets are ~95-97% new designs. We cap reprints at 15 across the entire 261-card set. Reprints should only occupy utility slots where the "right card" already exists (basic removal, combat tricks, mana dorks, etc.) — never set-identity cards. This limit does NOT include any bonus sheet, which is a separate card pool of reprints outside the main set numbering.

## Structural Infrastructure Checklist

- [ ] Mana fixing: How many dual lands? At what rarities?
- [ ] Mana sinks: Cards that use excess mana in late game?
- [ ] Deck smoothing: Scry, card draw, cycling, or equivalent?
- [ ] Evasion: Enough flying/menace/trample to break board stalls?
- [ ] Removal: Common removal in each color?
- [ ] Answers to bombs: Can commons/uncommons handle rares?
- [ ] Graveyard hate: If graveyard mechanics exist, are answers available?
- [ ] Artifact/enchantment removal: Sufficient answers in appropriate colors?
