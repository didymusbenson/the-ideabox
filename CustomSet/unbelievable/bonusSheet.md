### Power Level Guidelines

- **Uncommons (18):** Should play well in Limited without warping it. Solid role-players, archetype enablers, useful utility.
- **Rares (30):** Mix of exciting reprints and strong Limited cards. Some can be collector-excitement picks that don't play great in draft, but most should contribute.
- **Mythic Rares (15):** The chase cards. A few can be genuine bombs, but control the density — not every mythic should be a format-warping threat.
