# Reference Index

Quick guide to what each document covers and when to reach for it.

---

## Document Map

### sources.md — Raw Article Links
The original URLs to Mark Rosewater's Making Magic articles and other official MTG design resources. Use this when you need to go back to the actual source material for verification or deeper reading.

### fundamentals.md — Design Philosophy Synthesis
High-altitude synthesis of design principles across all 30+ articles. The "why" behind everything. Covers the Cardinal Rules, design philosophy, top-down design, keyword/mechanic philosophy, the creative process, card design principles, power level, and cross-cutting wisdom.

**Reach for this when:** Starting a new design conversation. Evaluating whether a direction feels right. Checking if a decision aligns with core principles. Resolving disagreements about design philosophy.

### nutsAndBolts.md — Step-by-Step Process Guide
Sequential walkthrough of HOW to design a set, from initial ideation through final polish. The "how" of set design. Follows the Nuts & Bolts series structure: ideation → card codes → skeleton → filling slots → finding mechanics → layering → limited design → playtesting → evaluation.

**Reach for this when:** Figuring out what to do next in the design process. Building or modifying the design skeleton. Planning playtesting. Deciding when to move from one design phase to the next.

### setAnatomy.md — Numbers and Specifications
The hard data: card counts by rarity, creature-to-spell ratios by color, mana curve distributions, booster pack contents, mechanic budgets, multicolor slot allocations, land slots. Verified against Nuts & Bolts articles.

**Reach for this when:** Building the design skeleton. Checking if your card counts are right. Verifying creature percentages per color. Calculating as-fan. Determining how many mechanics the set can support.

### archetypesAndThemes.md — Archetype Catalog
Comprehensive catalog of draft archetype structures (10 two-color pairs, guild sets, shards, wedges), historical examples of what each color pair has been across 13+ sets, 14 common set themes with their home colors, archetype speed balancing, and supporting-themes-at-common frameworks.

**Reach for this when:** Choosing draft archetypes for the set. Deciding on a faction structure. Picking set themes. Checking what a color pair has traditionally done. Balancing archetype speed. Determining enabler/payoff ratios.

### dosAndDonts.md — Practical Checklist
25 DOs, 24 DON'Ts, 12 GOTCHAs (counterintuitive traps), and 15 RED FLAGS (warning signs). Organized by design phase and category. Every entry cited to its source article.

**Reach for this when:** Reviewing a design decision before committing. Running a sanity check on the set. Diagnosing why something feels wrong. Checking for common mistakes. Evaluating whether the set is on track.

### bestPractices.md — Expert Wisdom
Rosewater's Twenty Lessons (complete list), the color pie as design constitution (what each color does/doesn't do), player psychographics (Timmy/Jenny/Spike/Vorthos/Mel), New World Order complexity management, the design pipeline, mechanic design best practices, the art of cutting, and balancing innovation with familiarity.

**Reach for this when:** Evaluating whether a mechanic respects the color pie. Checking if you're serving all player types. Managing complexity across rarities. Making hard cuts. Deciding how much innovation is enough.

### bonusSheets.md — Bonus Sheet Design Guide
How to design a bonus sheet: standard sizes (~63-65 cards), rarity splits (18-20 UC / 30 R / 15 MR), pack distribution models, thematic filter requirements, card selection criteria, Limited integration principles, bomb density control, and complete bonus sheet history. All principles sourced from Rosewater's State of Design columns, Storm Scale articles, Blogatog, and official Collecting articles.

**Reach for this when:** Deciding whether to include a bonus sheet. Choosing the thematic filter. Selecting reprint candidates. Evaluating bonus sheet impact on Limited. Determining pack distribution rate. Checking card pool depth for a proposed filter.
