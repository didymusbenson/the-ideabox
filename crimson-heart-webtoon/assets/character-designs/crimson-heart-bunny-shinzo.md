# Crimson Heart / Bunny Shinzo — Visual Reference

Design notes transcribed from the author's two model sheets. **Image files:**
`crimson-heart_teen-costume_ref.png`, `crimson-heart_adult-costume_ref.png` (add to this
folder). Canon-relevant details are also mirrored into the character sheet at
`../../loom/_books/crimson-heart/CHARACTERS/crimson-heart-bunny-shinzo.md`.

## Core look (both sheets)
- **Age 30.** Labeled "Former Magical Girl / stay-at-home mom."
- **Hair:** long, voluminous **curly auburn / red-brown** hair, side-parted with bangs.
- **Eyes:** warm brown. **Wears glasses** — thin-rimmed, and present in **both civilian
  and hero mode** (a signature; the magical girl who kept her glasses).
- **Build:** adult woman, curvy; warm skin tone.
- **Expression range** (from the sheets): confident, flustered/teasing, determined, soft,
  tired, annoyed, pensive, gentle smile — a wide, lived-in emotional register.
- **Signature magic FX:** pink/red heart-sparkle motes when casting.

## Costume A — TEEN COSTUME (original)
> Captioned "her old costume from when she was a teenager… **a little too tight now.**"

- Sleeveless **red halter crop top**, high mandarin collar with keyhole, gold trim, a red
  **heart gem** at the throat; bare midriff.
- **Red pleated skirt** over a **blue** underlayer, **white ruffle** hem, gold star accents.
- **White opera gloves.**
- **White thigh-high boots**, red heart at the knee, red heel; heart strap detail.
- **Accessories:** red heart brooch, red teardrop heart earrings, heart choker, back bow.
- Palette: bright red + royal **blue** + white + gold (classic magical-girl primary).

## Costume B — ADULT COSTUME (new, "designed for the woman she is now")
> Captioned "**Confident. Graceful. Still fighting for love and what matters.**"

- Deeper **crimson / wine** palette; gothic-elegant, grown-up.
- **Sheer red lace long sleeves**, off-shoulder neckline; red **heart gem** at the chest.
- **Black underbust corset/bodice** with lacing.
- **Asymmetric layered high-low skirt** (crimson with **black lace** trim), handkerchief hem.
- **Black lace-up high-heeled knee boots.**
- **Accessories:** draped **heart chain** at the hip, heart choker, heart earrings, heart
  wrist band.
- Palette: maroon/wine + red + **black** + white + gold + a magenta gem accent (the blue
  of the teen fit is dropped).

## Shared prop — the Heart Wand
- **Compact mode** (short) and **extended mode** (full staff): black shaft, gold fittings,
  a heart topper with red/blue gem set. Consistent across both costumes.
- (Reconcile with lore: her established transform item is the "transformation stick" in her
  purse — treat the **wand = the stick** unless we deliberately split them. Flagged in
  open-questions.)

## Story meaning — two costumes = character growth, NOT a power upgrade
The teen fit being **too tight** and a new design "for the woman she is now" is a gorgeous
visual for the whole premise: she can't squeeze back into her teenage self. **Keep this
aesthetic/emotional, not mechanical** — it does **not** violate the "endgame, no power
progression" rule. Suggested (unlocked, not locked): she suits up in the **old** costume in
the pilot (tight, a little absurd, a little poignant), and the **adult** costume is an
earned identity beat later. Placement is open — see open-questions.
