# Crimson Heart — Inspiration Playlist / Soundtrack

A living mood-board in song form. Tracks that capture a character's headspace, a scene's
energy, or the overall vibe — not a licensed soundtrack, just fuel for tone and drafting.

## How to use
- Tag each track with **who/what** it's for (a character, a scene, or the whole book).
- A one-line **why** keeps the intent legible when we come back to it.
- Nothing here is canon; it's texture.
