# Crimson Heart — Project Instructions

## What this is
A **webtoon** (serialized comic) — a magical-girl story about a retired, endgame-powered
heroine dragged back into the fight. We're authoring it here in loom's **book mode**.

## Status
Outlining. World bible and core cast are seeded; no scenes drafted yet.

## Genre
fantasy (magical girl / slice-of-life drama)

## Source of truth
The idea docs one level up are canon; this project mirrors them. Re-read when unsure:
- `../../premise.md` — pitch, themes, tone
- `../../roster.md` — the three suit-themed hero teams
- `../../antagonists.md` — the eight Tujeon Generals
- `../../pacing.md` — page-by-page opening + webtoon episode breaks
- `world.md` — the condensed story bible for this project
- `CHARACTERS/` — seeded cast sheets (authoritative for character detail)
- `OUTLINE.md` — chapter/episode roadmap to draft against

## Authoring approach (read this)
- This is **authored ensemble fiction**, not single-MC roleplay. `config.py` keeps
  `MC_NAME="MC"` (anonymous) on purpose so **no character is a protected PC** — develop
  the whole cast, Bunny included. POV is **all characters**.
- **Respect canon; flag, don't invent.** Where a sheet says **TBD** (names, ages,
  designs), leave it a gap or ask — don't silently fill it. Many names are deliberately
  unset.
- **Tone discipline:** fights are short and decisive; spend the pages on family, grief,
  and aftermath. Don't let action set-pieces sprawl.
- **Format awareness:** the target medium is webtoon (vertical scroll). `pacing.md`
  already breaks the opening into 6 episodes with cliffhangers — draft toward those beats.
