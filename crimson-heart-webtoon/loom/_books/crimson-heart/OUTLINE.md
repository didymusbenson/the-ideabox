## Opening arc — Chapters 1–2 ("Milk and Eggs")

Two ~19-page chapters → **6 webtoon episodes**, each ending on a hook. This is
Bunny's forced return: an ordinary day cracks open, the old life comes for her
daughter, and she suits back up.

> ⚠ **Pilot adjustment pending — cast is now ONE daughter (~8), not two.** The Ep. 1–6
> beats below still describe the **two-kid** draft (older kid to school; the *younger
> girl* in the car/store; a *4yo* watching TV at the end). Reblock for a single
> ~8-year-old. **Leading approach:** keep the **school drop-off** (kid safely out of the
> danger) and make **Kitty the menace** — either lurking through the drop-off with Bunny
> clocking her at the last minute, or shadowing the daughter *pre-contact* until Bunny
> intercepts. **Author owns this rewrite;** beats left as-is on purpose. Candidate
> approaches + affected files in `open-questions.md` → *Continuity*.

### Chapter 1
- **Ep. 1 — The bedtime story.** Cold-open on the "Final Crisis" flashback → reveal
  it's Bunny telling her daughters. Title-page couch beat: the "did they live happily
  ever after?" question over a panel of the dead love interest. Morning; the drive;
  the younger girl spots a shooting star. **Hook:** the star slams into a stranger.
- **Ep. 2 — Milk and eggs.** The grocery run; egg-price lament; the star-struck man
  makes a scene and is hauled out; the "probably drugs" talk with the kid. **Hook:**
  the cat speaks — "JUST RUN AWAY? IS THAT WHAT I TAUGHT YOU TO DO?"
- **Ep. 3 — It's all happening again.** Flee home; groceries; TV to distract the kid;
  anxiety attack and memory-flashes; call to the husband. **Hook:** he's caught in the
  Main Street chaos — crash and explosions over the phone.

### Chapter 2
- **Ep. 4 — No monsters.** Husband confirms he's safe; kid parked with the neighbor;
  the cat corners Bunny in the car; her refusal and grief ("I have a life now. NO
  MONSTERS!"). **Hook:** "That man you saw? That was no addict. It was the enemy."
- **Ep. 5 — Shine Heart Power.** Main Street; she spots her husband in the gridlock;
  the cat's appeal; digging the transformation stick out of her purse; resolve. **Hook:**
  the transformation splash — "SHINE HEART POWER! SPARKLE!"
- **Ep. 6 — Power of love.** The (very short) fight; the star shatters and frees the
  host; home; comfort on the couch; held-back tears. **Closer:** the milk gag.
