# The Daughter

## Identity
Bunny & Matty's **only child**, **~8 years old**. **Name: TBD.**
> **The resonance:** she's **the exact age Bunny was when Kitty first recruited her.** The
> cat coming for her *now* is the past arriving on schedule — that timing is the point.

## Personality — a little Crimson Heart
**Just like her mom:** **bold, kind, energetic, a helper.** The kid who runs toward the
person who needs help. It's exactly what makes her magic would-be-strong (a "pure heart"),
and exactly what terrifies Bunny — she recognizes herself.

## Her want (the engine of the family conflict)
- She **loves Bunny's bedtime stories about Crimson Heart** and thinks having magical
  powers would be **amazing.**
- She **wants to join up.** Genuinely, eagerly — to her it's all sparkles and glamour and
  helping people, none of the cost.
- Once the **real Crimson Heart** starts appearing (the downtown fight, the news), she's
  **determined to find out who she is and meet her** — not knowing it's her mother.
- **Bunny won't have it.** She refuses to let her kid walk into what she survived, *even
  though* to the daughter it looks like pure wonder. This is the central domestic engine:
  a child chasing the exact dream her mother is desperate to keep her from.

## Where it goes (planned beats — keep light)
- Bunny is forced to **tell Matty and her daughter the truth** about her past.
- Even then, Bunny **urges her not to let the cat enlist her** — the reveal doesn't end
  the conflict, it reframes it (now the kid knows the powers are *real* and within reach).

## Relationships
- **Bunny** — mother; her hero in the literal and unwitting sense. Loves the stories, wants the life Bunny is fighting to deny her.
- **Matty Shinzo** — father.
- **Kitty** — wants to recruit her; she'd say **yes** in a heartbeat if allowed.
