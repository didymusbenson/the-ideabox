# Jack (The Love Interest)

> **Working name.** "Jack" is a placeholder we're comfortable drafting with; may change.
> (Nice resonance with the deck motif — the Jack in the suits — but nothing is built on it.)

## Identity
Bunny's love interest from her magical-girl days — the story's **tuxedo-mask analog**:
the masked protector who steps in at the edge of a fight. Died in the final battle.

## The dynamic (we never see it on-page)
They had the classic **ego-rival / alter-ego / star-crossed** thing — pining across
secret identities, sparring as their public selves, two people who can't quite say it.
**We do not dramatize the relationship.** It lives in negative space; the reader feels
its shape through Bunny's grief, not through flashback romance.

## The sacrifice (unseen, undiscussed)
The final battle — like all of Bunny's original exploits — happens **off-page**. What
canon fixes:
- Ending it required Bunny's **ultimate combo with the other three senshi** (Amber
  Diamond, Onyx Club, Raven Spade) — see *the core four's combined ultimate* in world.md.
- **Jack stepped in and sacrificed himself** during that combo. Bunny saved everyone —
  **except him.**
- **Nothing in her power could undo it.** For the battle to end, his sacrifice had to be
  **permanent.** This is the one wound her magic — which fixes everything else in a blink
  — cannot touch. That's what makes it *the* wound.

## The whispered last words (a slow-burn secret)
Before he passed, Jack said **one cryptic final thing** to her.
- In every flashback and trauma-flashback it's rendered as **illegible, unintelligible
  speech** — no clear words, just the shape of him speaking.
- Bunny **cannot remember it** — not because she literally forgot, but because she
  **repressed** it: she couldn't understand or accept it at the time.
- It is a **real, fixed line** (author-held), **revealed over time** as she heals enough
  to hear it. Do **not** invent or paraphrase the content while drafting — keep it blank.

## How Bunny carries him
Not as a widow pining for a lost lover — **as a best friend and a loved one she mourns.**
The ache is grief + guilt (he died in *her* combo, saving *her*), not romantic longing.
Keep that register.

## What the husband knows
Her present-day husband knows only that she **had a high-school boyfriend who died.** He
does **not** know she was a magical girl at all at story start — so he has no idea who
Jack really was, or how he died.

## Relationships
- **Bunny** — beloved and lost; his irreversible death is the core of her trauma.
- **The core four (Diamond / Club / Spade)** — his sacrifice happened inside their
  combined ultimate; they carry it too.
- **The Husband** — knows Jack only as "a boyfriend who died."
