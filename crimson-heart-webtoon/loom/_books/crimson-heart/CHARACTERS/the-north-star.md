# The North Star

## Identity
General of the **Star (별)** suit in the Tujeon-based enemy. Crim's **first foe.**

## Personality
Held light — the enemy isn't deeply characterized. Manifests through possession rather than presence (so far).

## Background
MO: **falling-star possession** — a shooting star slams into a civilian and hijacks them (the 'star-struck man' in ch.1). Crim's first cleansing shatters the star and frees the host (ch.2 p.15). One of eight Tujeon Generals (see antagonists doc). **Design/appearance: TBD.**

## Relationships
- Commands the Star suit's rank-and-file (possessed civilians).
- First of the eight Generals Crim confronts.
