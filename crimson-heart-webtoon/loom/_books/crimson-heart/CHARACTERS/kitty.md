# Kitty

## Identity
The talking cat / mascot who recruits magical girls. Recruited Bunny long ago; now targeting her daughter. **True name/nature: TBD.**

## Personality
Snarky, blunt, guilt-trippy ('JUST RUN AWAY? IS THAT WHAT I TAUGHT YOU TO DO?'). Persistent — scratches at windows, won't be dismissed.

## Voice
Dry, needling, old-mentor cadence. Uses Bunny's name to cut through her denial.

## Background
Embodies the duty Bunny fled. Reveals the 'addict' from the store was the enemy, and pulls her back into the fight.

## Relationships
- **Bunny** — former (and would-be current) charge; she resents it.
- **The daughter (~8)** — recruitment target; the same age Bunny was when Kitty first recruited *her*. Unlike Bunny, the kid *wants* the calling.
