# Keyblade Warriors v05 — batch inspection

Reference: KH2 Final Mix Standard, level30 Sora, Kingdom Key, no optional combo or movement abilities. This is a working benchmark; we will choose the final Sora build later. Base stats STR21/MAG21/DEF18 include the weapon. HP20 deliberately excludes story bonuses. Soldier uses an explicit battle-level30 arena baseline, HP67. Magic actions are not implemented.

Launch **Play Keyblade Warriors v05.command**. WASD/mouse; Space jump; J or left click attack; R reset. The collision-view launcher adds cyan weapon spheres and red enemy attack spheres. The shapes are source gameplay volumes, so they extend beyond the visible blade. Start each check with R. The encounter waits for your first movement, jump or attack.

- [ ] **Kingdom Key:** inspect idle from several angles, then run, jump and attack. Gold guard, silver shaft/teeth, hilt and chain should be textured and remain attached to the hand. Flag any floating, mirrored, stretched or slipping weapon part. The old practice cube should be gone.
- [ ] **Loops and a single stroke:** run/stop repeatedly; let idle loop for at least10seconds. Tap attack once and leave it alone. The full followthrough should finish before ready; the Keyblade should remain aligned throughout. Note any seam, snap or cut-off.
- [ ] **Ground chain:** tap three times deliberately. First successor becomes available about.38sec into the first stroke, then.50sec into the second. Confirm two normal strokes then a distinct finisher. Mash through the end: a buffered press must not wrap the finisher into an endless combo. An attack after recovery starts a new chain. Early inputs more than roughly.13sec before the next window may expire.
- [ ] **Air chain:** jump and attack after leaving the floor, then continue the chain. Confirm three different air motions, ending with an air finisher, rather than reused ground swings. Check landing/interruption clears the chain. One jump is expected with optional movement abilities disabled.
- [ ] **Contacts:** attack facing away, just outside reach, above/below the Soldier, then close enough to connect. Only overlapping active source spheres should damage. Check startup/recovery do not damage. In the collision-view launcher, compare the cyan sweep to the actual Keyblade and the red sphere to the Soldier's attacking limb. Watch for hits through walls or unexplained distant hits.
- [ ] **Damage:** a fresh ground first hit should take Soldier67→62. When they connect separately, ground stroke powers100/65/150 produce5/4/8HP; air100/100/200 produce5/5/10HP. Each action hits this Soldier at most once. Missing should leave HP unchanged. The opponent may move or interrupt, so count actual contacts, not clicks.
- [ ] **Enemy and weapon ability:** stand close and allow a strike. Its source windup is about.97sec; its active window lasts about.22sec. First hit takes Sora20→9; with Kingdom Key Damage Control active below half HP, the next equal hit takes9→3. A subsequent hit can defeat Sora; Once More/Second Chance are not equipped.
- [ ] **Reset and repeat:** defeat either side, then press R. Confirm Sora20/20, Soldier67/67, zero hits, idle/weapon restored and no attack carried over. Repeat a few times; check camera, collision and input still behave normally.

Please report the checklist item, ground/air stage, what happened, and whether it repeats after R. A short recording is ideal for attachment/contact problems.

## What this batch establishes, and what still needs tuning

The actual Kingdom Key model, rig, textures and matching animation tracks are equipped. Source sphere radii, joints, weapon collision groups, ANB active/cancel windows, ATKP powers and finisher flags drive this route. Contacts are swept between samples to catch fast movement. Automated checks cover combo windows, terminal finishers, damage/ability boundaries, contact boundaries and fast sweeps; the packaged encounter also checks ground/air/enemy hits, death and restart.

This does not yet reproduce KH2's entire target-dependent attack selector, alternative finishers, lunge/root travel, enemy AI, revenge, hitstun/knockback or visual effects. Movement remains the prototype controller; animation root travel is removed. The input grace is configurable tuning. Damage units/rounding are reconstructed from extracted tables and direct reverse-engineering evidence, with an unresolved published divisor inconsistency; exact vanilla-game comparison remains outstanding. HP/story bonuses and the final ability build are intentionally undecided. Windows remains unbuilt and untested.
