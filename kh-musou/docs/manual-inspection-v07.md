# V07 movement/combat batch inspection

This is a controller reconstruction batch, not a complete port of KH2's original engine. The source audit explains the distinction. V04 remains the accepted appearance baseline; V05/V06/V07 have not received manual acceptance.

Open **Play Keyblade Warriors v07.command**. The older build was left running; close its window yourself when ready. Controls: WASD/mouse or gamepad; Space/bottom face button jumps; J/left click/left face button attacks; R resets. The collision-view launcher adds cyan attack spheres.

Keep one list of issues through the whole pass. For each, note ground/air, input held, nearby target position, whether the prior hit connected, and what you expected versus what occurred. No need to stop after each item.

1. **Run and change direction.** Run forward/back/sideways and diagonally, then release. Compare speed, braking and turn response. Diagonals should not add speed. This batch applies the source run parameter; acceleration/braking remain the Unreal adapter, and analog walk blending is still incomplete.
2. **Jump into fall and landing.** Jump standing still, then while moving. Watch the apex and the beginning of the fall pose. Fall should start with its own animation clock. Check landing recovery and immediate return to movement. Jump apex uses the source height; arc timing/air steering still need vanilla comparison.
3. **Hold movement during ground attacks.** Hold W, press J, keep W held through followthrough, then repeat with A/D. During the currently selected normal swings, attack root travel should drive position rather than adding full running speed. Facing follows timed target-turn windows. After completion, held movement should resume. Record sliding, snapping, late unlocks or lost inputs.
4. **Attack from several positions.** Try close front, close side and roughly two metres directly ahead. Watch the different strokes and lunge, early facing adjustment and full followthrough. Move relative to the target between strokes. Note incorrect selection or turning after an expected window closes. Full original target scoring and hard lock-on remain incomplete.
5. **Confirm hits and chain.** Against Soldier, press each follow-up near contact rather than holding the button. Check two normal actions then one finisher, distinct hit events and the end of the chain. Repeat into empty space: with Combo Master unequipped, a missed stroke should not advance the chain. Input buffer remains inferred.
6. **Air attacks while rising and falling.** Jump near Soldier, attack on ascent, then on descent. Try a confirmed aerial chain. Each stroke now applies its separate source vertical parameter and then gravity; it should not freeze height for the whole clip. Look for excessive bouncing, dropped hits, premature landing, strange steering or a finisher that feels wrong. Impulse conversion/order still need native confirmation.
7. **Interruptions, walls and restart.** Let Soldier interrupt a buffered action; no queued attack should replay afterward. Attack toward a wall and check that the capsule stops and the blocked target is unharmed. Land during an aerial action, then attack again on the ground. Press R and verify fresh health, enemy and control state.
8. **Appearance and collisions.** Use the collision-view launcher for an optional final pass. Watch the Keyblade grip, source textures, body deformation and cyan spheres during the whole stroke. Existing assets/materials are reused; no new visual acceptance is claimed from automated checks.

Known unfinished areas include native gravity/integration, full target/action selection, jump-hold/ceiling handling, analog walk animation, source knockback/recovery/iframes, enemy script execution and death/effects. This pass is intended to collect one correction batch, not to label those gaps solved.
